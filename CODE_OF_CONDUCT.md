# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; focus on what is best for the community.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery,
  doxxing, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing
the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers (see the repository's contact details or use
GitHub's reporting tools). All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove,
edit or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do not follow this code,
following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
