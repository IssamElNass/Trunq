A Tuesday, without Trunq.

  1. 09:00You open your database tool, then go get coffee while it loads.Trunq is open before your hand leaves the trackpad.
  2. 10:30A big report freezes the app. You force-quit.A million rows scroll like ten.
  3. 12:15Your update hangs. You stare at a spinner and guess.Trunq shows who's blocking you. One click and you're through.
  4. 14:00A "small" change locks the orders table. Checkout goes down.Trunq warns you first and shows the safe way to do it.
  5. 16:45You run a bulk update and hope for the best.You preview exactly what changes, then it's undone automatically.
  6. 18:00The server is full of forgotten connections. Again.Every connection was closed for you when you left.
